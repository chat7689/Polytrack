Ten courses cut into the hills, a coupe with real springs, dampers and tyres, and no luck anywhere in it. Start line to finish line, no checkpoints: brake for the corners, lean on the banking, land nose-up, and chase the ghost of your own best run.

Airborne, W and S pitch the nose down and up while A and D yaw it. Nothing auto-levels you: a kicker taken flat-out will put you into a real backflip, and it is your job to bring it round before the deck arrives.

Once you have set a time, a translucent ghost of that run replays alongside the next attempt and the HUD shows your live gap to it, measured at the point of the course you are actually on. There are no checkpoints and nothing random: identical inputs give an identical run, so every tenth is yours.

The courses are cut into the terrain, so running wide puts you on grass and down an embankment rather than into thin air. You can climb back on, but four seconds off the decking ends the run and returns you to the line.
