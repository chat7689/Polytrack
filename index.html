              How it works

              BasicsControlsMapsLeaderboards ReplaysCoachCreditsTrophies GarageAccount

              Basics

              Drive each map from the start to the finish gate as fast as you can. The clock starts when you first press a key and stops when your nose crosses the finish line. Your best time on each map goes on its leaderboard, to the thousandth of a second.

              Play on the menu starts the next map you haven't finished, or the one you rank worst on. You can pick a fixed map for it in Settings.

              Controls

              W / S
              Accelerate / brake, and reverse once stopped
              A / D
              Steer
              Space
              Boost. 3 seconds per run; it pushes harder the faster you're already going
              S + A / D
              At speed, braking while steering starts a drift. Hold the steering and throttle to keep it going
              Shift
              Handbrake
              In the air
              W / S tip the nose down / up, A / D turn the car
              R
              Restart the run
              T
              Telemetry (speed, g-forces, inputs)
              Esc
              Back one screen, all the way to the menu

              Off the deck you're on grass: slow, but you can drive back on. If the car is stuck or upside down, the run restarts by itself.

              Maps and sections

              There are 16 maps in four sections of four, A to D, getting harder as you go. Tight flat hairpins are faster drifted than braked for. Rollers and crests can lift the car off the road at speed.

              Leaderboards

              Courses lists every map with its top three and your best. Click a map for its full leaderboard, the podium and the map from above.

              The bar above each section opens that section's leaderboard: each driver's four best times added up. The Overall bar ranks everyone by maps finished first, then by the total of their best times.

              Watch, race and compare

              Every best time is saved with a ghost, a recording of the run. On a map's leaderboard, tick runs and choose:

              • Watch replays them together. The arrow keys switch between following one car, framing all of them and a free camera you fly with W A S D.
              • Race puts their ghosts on the track with you.
              • Compare (exactly two runs) shows the gap between them and graphs of distance, speed, acceleration and inputs.

              On a section's leaderboard, tick drivers who have times on all four maps. Watch or Compare then shows all four maps at once: a car finishing one map appears on the next, so the clock is their section total. On the Overall board the same works for the whole season: all 16 maps at once.

              Your own best on a map drives as a ghost beside you every time you retry it.

              Credits

              Finishing any map earns 1 credit. Spend them on colours in the Garage.

              Coach

              Coach on the menu is a chat about your racing. It knows every driver's best on every map, and answers with charts, photos and short clips beside the chat (each one numbered, in the colour it is mentioned in). Ask it which maps you can improve on fastest, where you lose time on a map, how you compare with a rival, or just say hello.

              The skill chart: each of its 16 spokes is a map, and the further out your point, the closer your best is to that map's #1 (the ring means you hold #1; the centre means twice their time). Coach alerts (in Settings) tell you when someone knocks you off a podium.

              Trophies

              Top three on a map, in a section or overall holds a trophy, engraved with its map. They're live: beat someone's time and their trophy is yours, until someone beats you. The trophy room also shows your credits, dropped in as gems.

              Garage

              A colour pack costs 10 credits and unlocks one new hue at random for the body or the trim. Saturation and brightness are free to change. Other players see your car in your colours in replays.

              Account

              You need an invite code to create an account. Your username is how you sign in and what shows on the leaderboards. There's no email, so if you forget your password, ask whoever runs the game to set a new one.

              In Settings you can turn sound on or off, pick what Play starts, change your password or reset your times. Credits stay when you reset.
